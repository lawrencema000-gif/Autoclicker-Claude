## Full Description (max 4000 chars)

Auto Clicker is a powerful, free automatic screen tapper for Android. Set up automated taps, swipes, and complex gesture patterns — then let the app do the work while you focus on what matters.

**4 Clicking Modes:**
- Single Tap — Pick one point, clicks repeat automatically
- Multi Tap — Set multiple points, clicks cycle through each one
- Pattern — Choose from 7 geometric shapes (circle, grid, spiral, zigzag, diamond, random, custom)
- Gesture Recording — Record your actual taps and swipes, then replay them perfectly

**Smart Anti-Detection:**
- Gaussian position randomization so no two taps hit the same pixel
- Natural timing variation with acceleration curves
- Human-like hold duration and micro-pauses
- Fatigue simulation for truly undetectable automation

**Control From Anywhere:**
- Volume button trigger — Start/pause/stop from any app without switching
- Floating bubble — Draggable play/pause button stays on screen
- Notification controls — Pause, resume, or stop from the notification bar
- 3-second countdown — Gives you time to switch apps before clicking starts

**Full Script System:**
- Save unlimited automation scripts
- Edit timing, loop count, and steps
- Organize with categories
- Import/export scripts as JSON
- Duplicate and share your best automations

**Customizable Speed:**
- 6 speed presets from Turbo (10ms) to Hourly
- Set exact intervals in milliseconds
- Configure tap hold duration and swipe speed
- Stop after X taps, X seconds, or X loops

**Built for Real Use:**
- Works with any app or game
- Floating toolbar shows live stats (tap count, elapsed time, loop counter)
- Battery optimization bypass keeps it running
- Light & dark theme with Material You support
- Runs reliably in the background

**How It Works:**
1. Enable the Accessibility Service (one-time setup)
2. Choose your mode and press START
3. Pick your target points on screen
4. Auto Clicker runs automatically — pause, resume, or stop anytime

No root required. No ads during clicking sessions. Your scripts and data stay on your device — nothing is uploaded.
