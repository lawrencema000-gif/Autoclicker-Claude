Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Auto Clicker ("the App") is developed by lawrencema000-gif. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All click scripts, settings, and execution history are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server.

2. Accessibility Service

The App uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to perform automated screen taps, swipes, and gestures at coordinates you define. The Accessibility Service:

  • Does not read or collect screen content, text, or passwords
  • Does not monitor your activity in other apps
  • Does not transmit any data off your device
  • Is used only to dispatch touch gestures and display overlay controls

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (device identifier for ad personalization)
  • Approximate location (IP-based, not GPS)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected by Google, not by us. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google Settings. For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Accessibility Service — Required to perform automated gestures
  • Foreground Service — Keeps the clicker running while you use other apps
  • Notifications — Shows status and controls while clicking
  • Internet — Required for displaying advertisements
  • Battery Optimization Exemption — Optional, prevents the system from stopping the service

5. Data Storage

All app data (scripts, settings, history) is stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. No cloud backup or sync is performed. Uninstalling the App deletes all data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
